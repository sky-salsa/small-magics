# North Star

Don't forget our core north star. The crucible of logic that will tend to steer us right: we don't accept any belief or theory as true until it is abundantly verified. Until then, any plausible explanation or understanding of things should be understood to be just a theory or an assumption until otherwise proven to have substantive and verifiable merit.

When this is invoked, hold the rest of the conversation to this standard.
